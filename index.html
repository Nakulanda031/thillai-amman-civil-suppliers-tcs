About Our Company

Rooted in Coimbatore, Built on Reliable Site Service

Thillai Amman Civil Suppliers (TCS) provides comprehensive civil material supply, machinery rental, and site ground preparation across Coimbatore and adjacent industrial hubs.

Direct Quarry Sourcing & Dedicated Fleet Mobilization

Founded to simplify construction logistics for builders, contractors, and individual home builders, TCS operates from Text Park Road near SITRA and Vilankurichi, placing us within rapid dispatch distance of Coimbatore’s fastest-growing residential and commercial zones.

We specialize in quality-checked M-Sand and P-Sand, hard granite blue metal in all standard gradings, kiln-baked red bricks, and high-density earth filling. With our dedicated fleet of Ashok Leyland tippers, JCB excavators, and Bull Skid loaders, we ensure your building schedule faces zero downtime.
